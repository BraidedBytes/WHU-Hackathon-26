# Spoilsport

Spoilsport helps people discover films while shielding them from plot reveals for films they plan to watch.

## Language

**Watchlist**:
A person's collection of films they plan to watch or have marked watched. It determines which watchlist films need spoiler protection.

**Protected film**:
A watchlist film that the person still plans to watch. Discussion that may reveal its plot is shielded.
_Avoid_: Unwatched film (a film can be unwatched without being on the watchlist)

**Extra shield**:
A film title added directly in the extension for spoiler protection without adding it to the watchlist. It is separate from the taste profile.

**Spoiler candidate**:
A passage that refers to a protected film or a film covered by an extra shield and may reveal plot beyond the premise. It stays obscured until judged safe or the person chooses to reveal it.
_Avoid_: Spoiler (until the passage has been judged)

**Taste profile**:
A person's film preferences used to shape spoiler-safe advice and recommendations. It is distinct from the watchlist.
